
An open source prototype for public annotation of archival objects, developed at Archives at NCBS with partners. 

**Key features:**  

- Enables public annotations on material imported from existing archival catalogues (EAD 2002 standard)  
- Annotations align with archival fields: subjects, people, places, description, plus a new "affect" field  
- Users can tag with existing controlled vocabulary or suggest new terms for a local vocabulary  
- Annotated objects can be exported back to source archives for richer catalogue descriptions  
- Source code: [https://github.com/tattle-made/archive-annotation/](https://github.com/tattle-made/archive-annotation/)  
- Launch video: [https://youtu.be/j8P2CIOjQUI](https://youtu.be/j8P2CIOjQUI)
