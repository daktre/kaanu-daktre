
**Mapeo** is part of the [Earth Defenders Toolkit](https://www.earthdefenderstoolkit.com/toolkit/mapeo-monitor-and-document-the-world-around-you/). Key features:

- Offline-first community mapping (designed for areas without connectivity)
- GPS points and photo documentation
- Built specifically for mapping ancestral lands
- Reportedly easy and fun to use (per Randall's field testing)
- Terrastories is another tool in the same toolkit ecosystem

## Discussion Context

ASD shared this on 12 Jun 2026 . 

- **Use case**: 12 forest-associated Adivasi communities in Karnataka (Aranya Aadhaarita Budakattu, Soliga, and others). Could the state Adivasi collective use Mapeo to map and claim ancestral lands?
- **FRA claims**: The Forest Rights Act claim process requires documented evidence. Mapeo's GPS points and photos could directly support individual (IFR) and community (CFR) claims.
- **Parallel in India**: ATREE recently relaunched their **Aamcho CFR Mapping App** - mobile-based GPS tracking for CFR mapping with real-time boundary plotting. They have facilitated CFR titles in Bastar. So the model is viable and already working in India.
- **Funding question**: Could we organise funding for the state Adivasi collective to do mapping and claiming work? Could this push the Adivasi land rights movement into the digital space?
## Kaanu Integration Possibilities

Kaanu already serves as an interpretive and archival platform for South Indian Adivasi studies. Key overlap points:

- **Community portraits**: Mapeo's GPS + photo data could feed into Kaanu's curated community portraits and exhibits, adding spatial and visual documentation to existing narrative and bibliographic layers.
- **Archival workflow**: ingesting mapped data as records. Mapeo exports could become Omeka S items? with Dublin Core metadata (location coordinates as `kaanu:identifier` or custom spatial fields?
- **Publications**: Kaanu already hosts Adivasi land rights publications (e.g., *Forest Conservation Amendment Act & Adivasi land rights 2025*, *Kerala Adivasi land claims & the welfare state 2025*). Mapeo mapping outputs could become linked exhibits or supplementary material for these?
