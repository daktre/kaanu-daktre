## Community-Wise Key Issues  

### Soliga  

- Need for mapping of community forest habitats, markers, and resource conservation  
- Restrictions under Tiger Project, Elephant Corridor, eco-sensitive zones, oxygen trading, fores enterprise are impacting livelihoods  
- Forest Rights Act 2026: land and habitation claims need processing  
- Skill training, capacity building, forest produce value addition, and market access needed 
- Misuse of Soliga caste certificates by non-tribals to access education, employment, and scheme benefits  

### Iruliga  

- Need for ethnographic study report on Iruliga community  
- Nutritional food distribution
- Basic infrastructure: housing, drinking water, roads  
- Prevention of caste certificate misuse  

### Jenu Kuruba  

- Staff shortages in Valmiki Tribal Ashram Schools leading to learning gaps and dropout  
- 3418 Adivasis evicted from Nagarahole forest need land and resettlement (per Dr. Muzaffar Asadi High Court Committee recommendations)  
- Misuse of government funds allocated for PVTG communities  
- Closure of PVTG Community Development Committees (CDC) and Jenu Kuruba district offices  
- Legal barriers to building houses in forest areas  

### Betta Kuruba  

- Demand to use the term "Betta Kuruba" specifically to prevent misuse of "Kadu Kuruba" caste certificates for political power and employment  
- Direct recruitment of educated tribal youth into civil services (per state budget announcement)  
- Implementation of recommendations from Lokur, Mohammed Pir, Dr. Muzaffar Asadi, and Dilip Singh Buriya committees  
- Implementation of 5th Schedule declaration and Panchayat Extension to Scheduled Areas (PESA) Act  
- Strengthening LAMPS cooperatives, TRIFED for minor forest produce processing, value addition, and marketing  
- Need for anthropological study to declare 9 forest-dependent communities as Adivasi  

### Siddi, Kudiya, Goudlu  

- Permanent teacher recruitment for Valmiki Tribal Ashram Schools, counselling for children, parent awareness programs  
- Skill training for youth with truncated education  
- Basic infrastructure (bridges, housing, electricity, drinking water) in Goudlu areas 
- Kodagu Kudiya community issues are unique due to hilltop settlements  
- Documentation of Kudiya freedom struggle leaders' history needed  
- Concerns about non-tribal communities attempting to enter ST list in Kodagu, threatening smaller Kudiya community  

### Hasalaru  

- Confusion between "Hasalaru" and "Hasla" terms despite shared culture and practices, resulting in different caste certificates  
- Community forest rights titles issued but forest officials restricting access  
- Land rights claims being rejected; forest and revenue departments refusing rights  
- Need for political representation from panchayat to Lok Sabha level for indigenous communities  
- Forest department employment concessions exclude Siddi and Hasalaru communities  
- Ethnographic study in Shivamogga and Chikkamagaluru districts to be extended to all taluks  
- Social welfare/ITDP officer non-cooperation hindering community work  

### Yerava  

- Need for Big Data system at TRI for information collection and preservation of indigenous communities      
- Community-led learning centres at hamlet, gram panchayat, taluk, and district levels  
- Coaching classes for competitive exam preparation  
- Establishment of EMRS or Navodaya-model residential school in Yerava language in high-population areas  
- Study needed on COVID-19 learning loss impact on tribal children's education  

## TRI Urgent Study Recommendations  

1. Study measures used by neighbouring states to prevent fake ST caste certificates  
2. Complete pnding ethnographic studies within time limits  
3. Prepare evidence-based reports for declaring forest-dependent communities as Adivasi  
4. Comparative study of education across ashram, Murarji, Eklavya, government, and private schools  
5. Impact study of nutritional food schemes on community health  
6. Study on barriers to Forest Rights Act implementation  
7. Study on education, health, employment, and economic status of resettled Adivasi families  
8. Study on feasibility of Schedule Area declaration and PESA implementation
